# Photo credits

City and country photos come from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com) under the
[Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/) (free to use, no attribution required).
Each record's `photo.source_url` in `data/countries.json` and `data/cities.json` links to the original.
They are resized to 960×640 WebP and served from this folder.
