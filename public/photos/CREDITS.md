# Photo credits

Country and city photos come from Wikimedia Commons (Creative Commons licences, credited) and Pexels
(Pexels License). The live site lists every photographer and licence at /en/credits, and each country page
shows its photo credit. Sources: data/country-guides.json, data/country-photos.json, data/countries.json,
data/cities.json. Photos are resized to 960×640 WebP and served from this folder.
