Most European programs start in September or October, and deadlines can be 6–9 months earlier. From Bangladesh, a few steps take longer than students expect.

## Start early with

- **Your passport.** Renewing it can take weeks, and you need it to apply.
- **IELTS or another test.** Book a date early; results take time, and you may want a second try.
- **Document attestation.** Certificates often need several offices. Allow a month or more.
- **Recommendation letters.** Teachers are busy near deadlines.

## After the offer

- Open a student file at your bank and pay what the university asks.
- Book the visa appointment as soon as you can; waiting times can be long.
- Arrange housing, then the flight and travel insurance.
