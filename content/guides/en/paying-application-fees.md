Application fees are usually small, but paying from Bangladesh can be confusing. This is general guidance; rules and limits change, so confirm with your bank first.

## Common ways to pay

- **International debit or credit card.** Many portals take card payments. Ask your bank to enable international online payments and check any yearly limit.
- **Bank transfer.** Some universities send bank details for a transfer. Your bank may ask for the university's invoice or offer letter.
- **Payment services the university names.** Use only the method listed on the official portal.

## Stay safe

- Pay only on the university's official portal or to the account in its official email.
- Never send money to a personal account, even if an agent asks.
- Keep the receipt and the confirmation email until you have your final decision.
- Application fees are rarely refunded, so apply only where you meet the requirements. The Fit Score's eligibility check helps here.
