The Fit Score compares your profile with a program's **published requirements**. It is not a chance of admission. Universities also look at motivation letters, interviews and how many people apply.

## Step 1: the eligibility gate

Before any score, we check the hard rules. If one fails, you see **Not eligible** and the reason:

- the program doesn't accept 3-year bachelor's degrees and yours is 3 years
- your field isn't one the program accepts
- you have no English score that meets the minimum, and MOI isn't accepted (or you don't have one)
- your CGPA is below the minimum after converting scales

## Step 2: the score

| Part | Points | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Academics | 40 | 20 for meeting the minimum, up to 20 more the further above it you are |
| English | 20 | 12 for meeting the minimum, 4 more per half IELTS band above it (MOI alone counts 12) |
| Field | 15 | 15 same field, 10 closely related, 5 loosely related |
| Experience | 15 | 1 point per 2 months of work or internships (max 10), 5 for research |
| Extras | 10 | 2 per relevant activity, certificate or leadership role |

80 or more is **Strong**, 60–79 **Good**, 40–59 **Possible** and below 40 **Weak**.

## Step 3: tips

We recalculate with one change at a time, such as half an IELTS band more, and show what would add the most points.

Always check the official program page before you decide.
