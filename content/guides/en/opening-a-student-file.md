To send tuition and living costs abroad legally, you usually open a **student file** at a bank in Bangladesh that handles foreign exchange. This is general guidance; ask your bank for its current list.

## What it is for

- Paying tuition, deposits and accommodation abroad
- Sending living costs each semester
- Keeping a record that helps with your visa and later transfers

## What banks usually ask for

- Your passport
- The admission or offer letter
- The university's fee details or invoice
- Your academic certificates
- Photos, and sometimes a parent's or sponsor's documents

## Tips

- Visit two or three banks: service, fees and processing times differ.
- Start soon after you get your offer, because the visa often needs proof of payment.
- Keep copies of every transfer receipt.
